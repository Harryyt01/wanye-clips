FAQ

Questions creators ask

Do you need my raw files?

No. A link is enough — YouTube, Twitch, Drive, Riverside or Zoom recording. If a video is unlisted, share it with my email and I can access it.

Who owns the clips?

You do, 100%. They are edits of your content, delivered for your channels. I don't repost them anywhere.

Can you match my brand style?

Yes — send one example clip or your colours/font and I'll match caption style, colours and pacing across the batch.

How do I pay, and when?

Cryptocurrency is recommended — USDT on TRC20, ERC20, BEP20 or another network we agree on (the exact coin and network are always confirmed with you first). PayPal, Razorpay and Payoneer are also accepted. First order: 50% to start, 50% when files are delivered. No payment is taken through this page — I confirm the quote by email first, then share verified payment instructions for the method you choose.

What if I don't like a clip?

Every clip includes one revision — different moment, tighter cut, caption or music fixes. If a trial clip completely misses, tell me why and I'll re-cut it once free.

Do you guarantee views?

No — nobody honestly can. What I guarantee is the craft: a real hook, clean captions, no dead air, and a clip watched end-to-end before it reaches you. Views depend on your content and posting consistency.
