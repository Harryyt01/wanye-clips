How a clip is built

38sPodcast
“Nobody tells you this about money…”

Podcast insight → 38s Short. Source: 52-minute interview.

Hook (0–3s): the guest's most contrarian line, cold open.
Middle: one idea only, cut every 2–4s, captions carry the argument muted.
End: payoff line + 0.5s loop-back to the hook so it replays cleanly.

Services

What every clip includes

Not just cut. Rebuilt for vertical. A clip is not a crop — every deliverable is re-edited for a phone screen and a 3-second attention window.

01

Hook-first edit

The strongest line moves to second 0–3. No intros, no “hey guys”, no dead air. If it doesn't earn the next second, it's cut.

02

Burned-in captions, styled to you

Word-by-word captions in your colours, readable muted — because most Shorts are watched on silent first.

03

Reframed, not letterboxed

Face-tracked 9:16 framing, 1080×1920, no black bars, no cut-off faces. Speaker stays centred when they move.

04

Sound that holds attention

Cleaned voice, levelled loudness, subtle music and whoosh / pop SFX on cuts — never louder than the voice.

05

Title + caption + hashtags

Each clip ships with a post-ready title, caption and 5–8 hashtags for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. You just upload.

06

One revision included

Wrong moment, caption typo, music too loud? One round of fixes per clip is included in every package.

FAQ

Questions creators ask

Do you need my raw files?

No. A link is enough — YouTube, Twitch, Drive, Riverside or Zoom recording. If a video is unlisted, share it with my email and I can access it.

Who owns the clips?

You do, 100%. They are edits of your content, delivered for your channels. I don't repost them anywhere.

Can you match my brand style?

Yes — send one example clip or your colours/font and I'll match caption style, colours and pacing across the batch.

How do I pay, and when?

PayPal, Payoneer or Wise. First order: 50% to start, 50% when files are delivered. No payment is taken through this page — I confirm the quote by email first.

What if I don't like a clip?

Every clip includes one revision — different moment, tighter cut, caption or music fixes. If a trial clip completely misses, tell me why and I'll re-cut it once free.

Do you guarantee views?

No — nobody honestly can. What I guarantee is the craft: a real hook, clean captions, no dead air, and a clip watched end-to-end before it reaches you. Views depend on your content and posting consistency.
